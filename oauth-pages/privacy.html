Privacy Policy for BGW ERP Backup

Last updated: 15 November 2026

BGW ERP Backup is an internal data redundancy tool developed for Bharti's Green World Nursery & Landscaping, Kolhapur.

  • Exclusive Cloud Purpose: The application uses the nursery owner's Google Drive account only to upload the nursery's own internal database backup files.
  • No Personal Data Collection or Sharing: The application does not collect, sell, monetize, or share personal data with third parties.
  • Owner-Only Access: BGW ERP Backup is a private operational utility used solely by the nursery owner to safeguard botanical inventory and billing data against loss.
Contact: For questions regarding this policy or data safety, email us at bhartisgreenworld@gmail.com.
