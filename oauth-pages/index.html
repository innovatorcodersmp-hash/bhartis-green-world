BGW ERP Backup

BGW ERP Backup is an internal tool of Bharti's Green World Nursery & Landscaping, Kolhapur. The application automatically backs up the nursery's own business database (plant stock, retail counter bills, vendor records, and landscaping contracts) directly to the nursery owner's Google Drive account for disaster recovery.
